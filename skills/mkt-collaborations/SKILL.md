# Collaborations (no cash)

## Partner types
- **Micro/nano creators** (1k–50k followers) whose audience matches yours — trade product/service, experience, or exposure.
- **Complementary businesses** (same customer, different product): bakery × florist, gym × nutritionist, coaching × stationery.
- **Peers** in the niche for joint content: debates, duets, swaps.
- **Local communities**: colleges, clubs, events, NGOs.

## Find and vet
Search hashtags/keywords/location; check real engagement (comments that are genuine), audience location, brand fit, past paid-partnership disclosures.

## Outreach DM/email (short)
Compliment something specific → idea that benefits their audience → what you offer → simple next step. No mass copy-paste.

## Collab formats
Instagram Collab posts (shows on both profiles), joint Reels, takeover, joint live, co-created guide, bundle offer, giveaway (`mkt-giveaways`), guest video on each other's channel.

## Agreement (write it down)
Deliverables, dates, usage rights for content, disclosure (#collab/#ad as required), what each side gives.

## Track
Partner | audience size | format | date | reach | follows | leads/sales.

## Rules
Disclose partnerships per platform rules and advertising guidelines (e.g. ASCI in India, FTC in the US). Collab posts and outreach are drafts until the user approves.
