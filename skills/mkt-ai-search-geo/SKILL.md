# AI search visibility (GEO)

GEO extends SEO rather than replacing it: Google says its AI features are rooted in core Search ranking systems, and AI answers often draw from pages that already rank well. Clicks from AI Overviews have fallen for many queries, so track citations and brand mentions too.

## Actions
1. **Strong SEO base** (`mkt-seo-audit`, `mkt-blog-article`).
2. **Answer-first content**: clear 40–60 word answers under question headings; definitions, steps, comparisons and tables that are easy to quote.
3. **Original facts**: your own data, prices, case results, surveys — things only you can say.
4. **Entity clarity**: consistent brand name, description, address and links across website, Google Business Profile, social profiles, directories; Organization/LocalBusiness schema.
5. **Third-party mentions**: reviews, listicles, local news, industry sites, Reddit/Quora discussions (`mkt-community-forums`, `mkt-digital-pr`).
6. **Crawlable pages**: important text in HTML, not only behind scripts.
7. **Freshness**: update key pages with dates and new information.

## Monitor (monthly)
Ask AI assistants the top 20 customer questions; record whether the brand is mentioned/cited and which sources are cited instead; track in a sheet; target those sources.

## Rules
No fake reviews, spammy self-promotional posts, or manipulative hidden text.
