# Market expansion and opportunity research

## 1. Define the opportunity
Segment/geography/product, why now, hypothesis of value.

## 2. Size it (top-down + bottom-up)
- Top-down: population/businesses in the segment × % who need it × price.
- Bottom-up: reachable customers × realistic conversion × average order × frequency.
Show assumptions; label estimates.

## 3. Demand signals (free research)
Search volume and trends, marketplace listings and reviews, competitor presence, job posts, community questions, government/industry reports, interviews with 5–10 potential customers.

## 4. Competition
Who serves them now, prices, gaps, switching costs.

## 5. Entry options
Online-only, partner/distributor (`bd-channel-partners`), marketplace (`growth-marketplaces`), pop-up/pilot location, franchise, B2B tenders (`bd-tenders-gem`).

## 6. Pilot plan
Smallest test (4–8 weeks), budget/time, success metrics (e.g. 20 paying customers, CAC, margin), go/no-go criteria.

## Output
One-page opportunity brief with sources, sizing table, risks, recommendation and pilot plan. Use web research with links; never present guesses as facts.
