# Carousels and document posts

Carousels earn saves and shares (strong signals on Instagram) and document posts get long dwell time on LinkedIn.

## Structure (6–10 slides)
1. **Cover**: big hook (≤ 8 words) + visual cue to swipe.
2. **Problem**: why this matters / cost of getting it wrong.
3–8. **Value**: one idea per slide — step, tip, example, mini-case, checklist.
9. **Summary**: the whole thing on one slide (most-screenshotted).
10. **CTA**: save / share with a friend who needs this / follow / comment keyword.

## Copy rules
≤ 25 words per slide, large text, one font pair, brand colours, high contrast. Use numbers and concrete examples. Each slide should make the reader want the next.

## Design brief (for Canva or the bot's design tool)
Size: Instagram 1080×1350 (4:5); LinkedIn PDF 1080×1350 or 1920×1080; consistent template; logo/handle small on every slide.

## Caption
Hook line + 2–3 lines of context + question to spark comments + 3–5 keywords.

## Output
Slide copy table (slide | headline | body | visual), caption, alt text, and a design brief or a generated file if a design integration is connected.
