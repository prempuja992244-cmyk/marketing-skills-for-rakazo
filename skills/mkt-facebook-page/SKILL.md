# Facebook Page (organic)

Organic reach for business Pages is low on average (many reports put it in the low single-digit percent of followers), so Pages win with video/Reels, conversation, Groups and original content.

## Setup
Category, clear profile photo/logo, cover with promise, intro (255 chars) with keywords, contact/WhatsApp button, location and hours (local), services/shop, username matching other socials.

## Content mix per week
- 3–5 **Reels** (native upload; Reels are a main organic reach driver).
- 2 **photo/album** posts (real people, products, behind the scenes).
- 1–2 **conversation posts** (question, poll, this-or-that) — reply to every comment, ideally within the first hour.
- 1 **Live** every week or two (Q&A, demo, launch).
- Links sparingly: put links in the first comment or 1–2 link posts a month.

## Habits
- **Original content**: Meta favours original creators; add your own edit or commentary if reposting.
- **Refresh winners**: re-edit and repost your best content from 6–12 months ago.
- Share Page posts into your relevant Group (`mkt-facebook-groups`) and encourage team members to share from personal profiles.

## Automation
Draft and schedule via the connected Facebook Pages integration or Meta Business Suite; reply drafts via `mkt-auto-engagement`.
