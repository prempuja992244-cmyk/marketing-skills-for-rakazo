# Facebook Groups

Groups generally get stronger organic reach than Pages because Facebook prioritises community conversations.

## Own Group setup
- Name with the community promise + keyword ("Pune Home Bakers – Tips & Orders Help").
- Linked to the Page; membership questions (what do you want help with? email for free guide — optional with consent).
- Rules: be kind, no spam, promo only on promo day.
- Welcome post each week tagging new members; pinned "start here" post with best resources.

## Weekly themes
Mon tip · Tue question/poll · Wed wins · Thu live Q&A · Fri promo thread (members share their offers) · weekend story.

## Engagement routine
Answer every question, turn repeated questions into posts/videos, spotlight members, run small challenges (5-day challenge).

## Participating in others' Groups
Read rules; answer questions with real value; no links unless allowed; let your profile and helpful answers bring people to you. No mass-posting the same message in many groups.

## Automation
Bot drafts daily theme posts, welcome messages and answers; the user/admin approves. Membership approvals and moderation decisions stay human unless rules are crystal clear.
