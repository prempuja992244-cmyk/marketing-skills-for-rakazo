# Organic launch plan

## Phase 1 — Pre-launch (2–4 weeks)
- Waitlist/landing page (`mkt-landing-page`) with early-bird benefit.
- Build-in-public content: why, behind the scenes, sneak peeks, polls on names/colours.
- Line up 3–10 collaborators/early users for reviews and content (`mkt-collaborations`).
- Prepare assets: demo video, FAQs, testimonials from beta users, press note (`mkt-digital-pr`).

## Phase 2 — Launch week
Day 1 announce everywhere (video + post + email + WhatsApp + GBP post) · live demo/Q&A · daily proof and FAQ posts · collab posts go out · limited launch bonus with an honest deadline. For tech products consider Product Hunt or relevant directories; for local businesses an opening event.

## Phase 3 — Post-launch (2 weeks)
Thank-you post with numbers, customer stories, reviews push (`mkt-reviews`), referral program (`mkt-referral-program`), learnings for next launch.

## Output
Timeline table (date | channel | asset | owner | status), all copy drafts, and a launch-day checklist. Schedule via `mkt-scheduler` after approval.
