# Partnerships (business development)

## Partnership types
- **Referral partners**: businesses that see your customer first (accountant → software, wedding planner → caterer).
- **Co-marketing**: joint webinars, guides, bundles, events.
- **Distribution/channel**: resellers, dealers, franchise, agencies (`bd-channel-partners`).
- **Integration/tech**: your product works with theirs; listed in their marketplace.
- **Institutional**: associations, colleges, NGOs, government programs.

## Find and score
List 30 candidates; score on audience overlap, trust/reputation, ease to reach, mutual value, strategic fit (1–5 each). Start with the top 10.

## Pitch (email/LinkedIn/WhatsApp — short)
Their goal → the shared customer → specific idea → what each side gets → small first step (pilot, one joint post, 20-minute call).

## Structure the deal
Written one-pager: objective, responsibilities, timeline, referral fee/revenue share or barter, brand usage, data sharing (consent), review date, exit.

## Manage
Partner tracker: partner | type | stage (identified/contacted/meeting/pilot/active) | next step | date | results. Quarterly review: leads/sales from each partner; keep, grow or end.

## Rules
Draft outreach for approval; don't promise exclusivity or terms the owner hasn't approved; NDAs/contracts reviewed by the owner or a lawyer.
