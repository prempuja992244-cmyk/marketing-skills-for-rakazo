# Platform rules and compliance

This is practical guidance, not legal advice.

## Automation
- Use official APIs/integrations and native schedulers. No unofficial bots that mass-follow, mass-like, mass-comment or scrape; no buying followers/engagement; no multiple fake accounts. These lead to reach limits or bans.
- Human pace for any browser-based actions; stop at login checks/CAPTCHAs and ask the user.

## Content
- Music: platform libraries or licensed tracks only; business accounts may have restricted libraries.
- Originality: Meta and YouTube reduce distribution for unoriginal reposts; transform and credit.
- AI: label realistic AI-generated or altered media where platforms require (YouTube, Meta).
- Claims: no false, misleading or unprovable claims; extra care for health, finance, education results.

## Disclosures
Paid or gifted collaborations must be disclosed (platform paid-partnership tools + #ad/#collab) per local rules such as ASCI influencer guidelines (India) or FTC (US).

## Messaging and email
Opt-in only; easy unsubscribe; honour opt-outs; follow Gmail/Yahoo sender rules (`mkt-email-deliverability`), WhatsApp Business policy, and data laws (DPDP Act in India, GDPR in EU/UK, CAN-SPAM/TCPA in US).

## Contests
Follow platform promotion rules and local contest laws (`mkt-giveaways`).
