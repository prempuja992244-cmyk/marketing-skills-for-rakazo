# Organic content strategy

## 1. Pick channels (focus beats spread)
Choose **1 discovery channel + 1 conversion channel + 1 owned channel** to start, based on audience and capacity:
- Discovery (reach new people): YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels / LinkedIn personal profile / Pinterest / SEO.
- Conversion/trust: YouTube long-form, LinkedIn posts, Facebook Group, website/blog, Google Business Profile.
- Owned (no algorithm risk): email newsletter, WhatsApp channel/community.
Add more channels only after the first three run consistently for 30 days.

## 2. Content pillars (3–5)
Each pillar ties a customer pain/desire (`audience.md`) to your expertise. Mix:
- **Teach** (how-to, mistakes, checklists) — saves and shares.
- **Show** (behind the scenes, process, before/after) — trust.
- **Prove** (results, testimonials, case studies) — conversion.
- **Connect** (story, opinion, founder journey) — follows and comments.
- **Sell** (offer, demo, FAQ) — ~1 in 5 posts.

## 3. Cadence (sustainable minimum)
Short video 3–5/week · long video 1/week or 2/month · LinkedIn 3/week · email 1/week · GBP post 1/week · stories daily if possible. Consistency over volume.

## 4. Funnel
Short video → profile/bio link → lead magnet (`mkt-lead-magnet`) → email/WhatsApp nurture → offer. Every post has one next step.

## 5. 90-day plan
Month 1 setup + consistency; month 2 double down on top formats; month 3 collaborations and repurposing at scale. Weekly review via `mkt-analytics-report`.

## Output
`content-strategy.md`: channels and why, pillars with example topics, format mix, cadence, funnel, KPIs, 90-day milestones.
