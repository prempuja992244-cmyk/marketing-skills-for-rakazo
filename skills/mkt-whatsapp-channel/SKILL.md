# WhatsApp and Telegram channels

## Options
- **WhatsApp Channel**: one-way updates, people follow freely (no phone numbers shown). Great for offers, new arrivals, tips.
- **WhatsApp Community / Groups**: two-way, for customers or members.
- **WhatsApp Business broadcasts / API messages**: only to contacts who opted in; templates for messages outside the 24-hour window.
- **Telegram channel**: popular for education, deals, and tech audiences.

## Grow subscribers (free)
Link in all bios, QR code at the shop/packaging/invoices, end of every video ("follow the WhatsApp channel for…"), email footer, website pop-up, lead magnet delivery.

## Content rhythm
3–5 updates/week: 1 tip, 1 behind-the-scenes, 1 offer/new product, 1 poll or question, 1 proof (review). Short text + image/video; one link max.

## Rules
- Never add people to groups or broadcasts without consent; never message scraped/bought numbers.
- Honour "stop" instantly; follow WhatsApp Business policy and local telecom/data rules (e.g. India's DPDP Act and TRAI rules for promotional messages).

## Automation
Bot drafts the week's updates and schedules/sends via a connected WhatsApp Business integration after approval; Channels may need the user's own app session.
