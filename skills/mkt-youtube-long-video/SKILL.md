# Long-form YouTube video

YouTube increasingly weighs viewer satisfaction (surveys, likes, return visits), not just raw watch time. Deliver the title's promise fast and fully.

## Outline
1. **Cold open (0–30s)**: restate the promise from the title/thumbnail, show the end result or stakes, say what the viewer will get. No long intro or logo.
2. **Context (≤ 60s)**: why it matters, who it's for.
3. **Body**: 3–7 sections in logical order; each starts with a mini-hook ("the mistake most people make here…"), delivers, then teases the next.
4. **Proof**: demo, screen share, example, numbers.
5. **Recap**: key points in 20 seconds.
6. **CTA**: one action — watch the next video (end screen), get the lead magnet, subscribe for the series.

## Retention tools
Change visuals every 5–15s (B-roll, zoom, text, screen), cut pauses, keep sentences short, show rather than tell, use chapters.

## Length
As long as needed, as short as possible. Don't pad.

## After publishing (first 48 hours)
Check retention graph dips → note what caused them for the next script; reply to early comments (`mkt-youtube-community`); post a Short that points to it; share to email/WhatsApp.

## Output
Full outline + spoken script (in the brand's language) + B-roll list + chapter list + pinned comment.
