# Storytelling content

## Story bank (build once, add weekly)
Ask the user for: why they started, hardest moment, a big mistake, a customer they'll never forget, a surprising win, a behind-the-scenes routine, a strong opinion about the industry. Save to `stories.md`.

## Story structure (short form)
1. **Hook**: drop into the moment ("At 2 a.m. the oven died — 40 cakes due at 9.").
2. **Stakes**: what could be lost.
3. **Turn**: the decision or discovery.
4. **Outcome**: what happened, with a concrete detail.
5. **Lesson**: one takeaway useful to the reader.
6. **Bridge** (optional): how this shapes what you offer now.

## Customer story (case study) structure
Who they are → problem in their words → what they tried → what changed with you → result with numbers → quote → CTA.

## Rules
- True stories only; get permission before naming customers or showing faces; blur private info.
- Raw and real beats polished: phone video, real photos, real numbers.
- One story = one lesson.
