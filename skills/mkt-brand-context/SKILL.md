# Brand context

## Check first
Look for `brand-context.md` in memory/workspace. If present, summarise it and ask only about gaps or changes.

## Collect (≤ 5 questions per message)
1. **Business**: name, what you sell, price range, where you sell (city/country/online), website and social links.
2. **Customer**: who buys, their age/role, top 3 problems, what they search for, where they hang out online.
3. **Promise and proof**: main benefit in one line, results, testimonials, numbers, awards (only real ones).
4. **Voice**: 3 adjectives (e.g. friendly, expert, bold), words to use/avoid, emoji yes/no, language (English/Hindi/Hinglish/regional).
5. **Channels**: which accounts exist (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Page/Group, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail list, blog, Google Business Profile).
6. **Goal for 90 days**: one main metric (followers, leads, store visits, sales, newsletter subscribers) and current baseline.
7. **Capacity**: who appears on camera, hours per week, existing photos/videos, design tool (Canva etc.).
8. **Rules**: claims not allowed, topics to avoid, approval needs.

## Save
Write `brand-context.md` with headings: Business, Customer, Promise & proof, Voice, Channels (with handles), Goal & baseline, Capacity, Rules. Save a 5-line summary to memory.

## Rules
Never invent proof, numbers or testimonials — mark unknowns `UNKNOWN`. Re-run when the offer, audience or goal changes.
