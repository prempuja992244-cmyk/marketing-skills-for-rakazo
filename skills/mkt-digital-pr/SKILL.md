# Free PR, mentions and links

## Angles that earn coverage
Original data/survey, local first or milestone, founder story with a strong hook, timely comment on news/trends, community initiative, unusual product use, expert tips for a season/festival.

## Channels
- Journalist request platforms (e.g. Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer, SourceBottle) — answer fast with a clear expert quote.
- Local newspapers, city blogs, community pages, college and business associations.
- Podcasts and YouTube channels in the niche (pitch a specific topic, not "interview me").
- Guest articles on industry sites; supplier/partner "customer stories" pages; awards and lists.
- Directories and chambers of commerce relevant to the business.

## Pitch template (≤ 120 words)
Subject: specific story angle · 1 line why it matters to their audience now · 2–3 facts/data · what you can provide (quote, photos, data) · contact.

## Track
Outlet | contact | angle | date | status | result/link.

## Rules
Pitch individuals relevant to the topic; no mass blasting; no paid links disguised as editorial; follow up once.
