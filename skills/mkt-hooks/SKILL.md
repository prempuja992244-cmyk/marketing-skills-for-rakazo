# Hooks

Retention in the first seconds drives distribution on Shorts, Reels and Facebook video, and the first line decides if a text post gets expanded. A hook makes a specific promise the content keeps.

## Hook patterns (pick the one that fits the content)
1. **Specific result**: "How we got 312 bookings from one Google profile."
2. **Mistake**: "Stop doing this when you price your cakes."
3. **Contrarian**: "Posting every day is killing your reach."
4. **Curiosity gap**: "The cheapest tool in my shop makes the most money."
5. **Direct callout**: "If you run a salon in Pune, watch this."
6. **Question with stakes**: "Why do 9 out of 10 home bakers quit in year one?"
7. **Before/after visual**: show the end result in frame 1.
8. **Story start**: "Last Diwali I almost shut the shop."
9. **List promise**: "3 free tools that replaced my designer."
10. **Myth bust**: "No, you don't need 10k followers to sell."

## For video
- Say the hook in the first 1–2 seconds AND put it as on-screen text.
- Start with motion or the result; no logo intros, no "hi guys welcome back".
- Match the visual to the words.

## Process
Write 10 hooks per idea → remove vague ones → pick 2 to A/B test (`mkt-experiments`). Check: specific? true? relevant to the persona? delivered in the content?

## Rules
No clickbait that the content doesn't deliver — it hurts satisfaction signals and trust. No fake urgency or false claims.
