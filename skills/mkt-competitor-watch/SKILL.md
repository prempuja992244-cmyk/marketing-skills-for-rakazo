# Competitor watch

## Pick 3–5 competitors
Direct competitors + 1–2 "aspirational" creators/brands in the niche.

## Collect (public data)
- Social: top 10 posts by views/engagement in last 90 days per platform; formats; posting frequency; hooks; CTAs.
- YouTube: outlier videos (views far above their average), titles/thumbnails patterns.
- SEO: pages ranking for our keywords, their content depth.
- Offers/pricing, lead magnets, launches.
- Reviews: what customers love/hate.

## Analyse
- What topics/formats consistently win?
- What do customers complain about → our positioning opportunity.
- What's missing (topics, formats, languages, local angles) → our gap.

## Output
Competitor table + 5 actions: content to create, formats to try, positioning lines, keywords to target, offer ideas. Monthly refresh as a routine.

## Rules
Learn from patterns; never copy content, designs or text.
