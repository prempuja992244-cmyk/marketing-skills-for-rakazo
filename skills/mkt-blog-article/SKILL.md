# SEO blog article

## Outline from intent
Search the main keyword; note what top results cover and what they miss. Plan an article that answers faster, more specifically, and with first-hand experience.

## Structure
- **Title** (≤ 60 chars, keyword early) and **meta description** (≤ 155 chars, benefit + CTA).
- **Direct answer** in the first 2–3 sentences (helps snippets and AI answers).
- H2/H3 sections matching sub-questions; short paragraphs; lists and tables.
- **Experience**: your photos, numbers, examples, mistakes, quotes from customers (with permission).
- **FAQ** section answering People Also Ask questions.
- **CTA**: lead magnet or product, placed mid-article and at the end.
- **Internal links** to 2–4 related pages; external links to credible sources.
- Author name and bio; updated date.

## On-page checklist
URL short with keyword; one H1; descriptive image alt text; compressed images; FAQ/Article schema where relevant; mobile readable.

## Publish and spread
Publish via the connected CMS (WordPress, Webflow, etc.) after approval; then repurpose (`mkt-repurpose`) and add to the newsletter.

## Rules
No mass-produced unedited AI text; Google's helpful-content and spam policies reward people-first content with real expertise.
