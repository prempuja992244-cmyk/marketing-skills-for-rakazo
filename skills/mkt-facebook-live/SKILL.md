# Live sessions

Lives tend to get extra distribution and notifications, and they build trust fast.

## Before (3–7 days)
- Topic that solves one specific problem; title with a clear promise.
- Schedule the live/event so followers can set reminders; announce on all channels, email and WhatsApp; Stories countdown.
- Prepare: run-of-show, 3 key points, demo/props, questions to seed, offer (if any), moderator.

## Run-of-show (30–45 min)
0–3 min: welcome, say what they'll get, ask where people are watching from.
3–25 min: content in 3 parts, pause for questions after each.
25–35 min: live Q&A.
35–40 min: recap + one CTA (lead magnet, booking, offer with honest terms).

## Engagement prompts
Ask yes/no questions, "type 1 if…", shout out names, answer comments live.

## After
Download replay → cut 5–10 clips (`mkt-repurpose`) → post replay link to email/WhatsApp → reply to all comments → note questions for future content.

## Checklist
Stable internet, phone on stand, light facing you, test audio, notifications off.
