# Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. Expert surveys (e.g. Whitespark 2026) rank the **primary GBP category** as the top factor, and reviews (count, rating, recency) are among the strongest signals.

## Profile setup (do fully)
- Exact business name (no keyword stuffing — it can get the profile suspended).
- **Primary category** = the most specific match for the main service; add relevant secondary categories.
- Address/service area, phone, website (link to a matching local page), hours incl. holidays.
- Services/products with descriptions and prices where possible; attributes.
- 20+ real photos (outside, inside, team, work, products), then add new ones weekly.
- Business description with what you do and where (750 chars).

## Weekly routine (automatable)
- 1 GBP post (offer, update, event, new work) with photo.
- Add 3–5 new photos.
- Reply to every review within 48h (`mkt-reviews`).
- Answer Q&A; add your own FAQs.
- Ask recent happy customers for reviews (short link/QR).

## Website and citations
Local landing page per main service/location with NAP (name, address, phone) matching GBP, embedded map, local proof; consistent listings on major directories (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Yelp, etc. as relevant to the country).

## Track
Calls, direction requests, website clicks, and rankings for 5–10 "service + area" searches monthly.
