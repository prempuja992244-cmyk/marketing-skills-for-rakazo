# Channel partners (resellers, dealers, affiliates)

## Ideal partner profile
Who already sells to your customers, in which regions, with what capacity; must-haves and red flags.

## Program design
- Margin/commission structure (protect your own profitability), minimum orders, territories (if any), payment terms.
- Partner tiers with benefits (marketing support, priority stock, higher margin).
- Rules: pricing policy, brand usage, returns, conflicts.

## Recruit
Lists from directories, IndiaMART/TradeIndia, trade fairs, LinkedIn, industry associations; outreach sequence (intro → value → call → sample/demo → agreement).

## Onboard (partner kit)
Product sheet, price list, pitch script, FAQs/objections, demo video, social media creatives, order process, contact for support.

## Enable and grow
Monthly partner newsletter, training sessions, co-branded content, leaderboard and incentives, quick support.

## Track
Partner | region | start date | orders | revenue | activity | issues | next action. Review monthly; help low performers or phase out.

## Rules
Clear written agreements; no promises of earnings to affiliates; disclose affiliate links per advertising rules.
