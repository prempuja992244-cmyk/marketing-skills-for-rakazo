# User-generated content (UGC)

## Encourage
- Branded hashtag or "tag us" note on packaging, receipts, thank-you messages.
- Make moments photo-worthy (packaging, setup, result).
- Ask happy customers for a 15-second video answering 3 prompts: before, what changed, who should try it.
- Feature customers regularly so others want to be featured.
- Small thank-yous (discount on next order, shout-out) — not payment for positive opinions.

## Collect
Monitor tags/mentions (`mkt-social-listening`), DMs, reviews, WhatsApp messages. Save to a UGC library with: creator, link, date, permission status, usage allowed.

## Permission
Ask in writing before reposting: "Love this! Can we share it on our page with credit to you?" Keep the reply as record. Credit the creator.

## Use
Reels compilations, carousel of reviews, website testimonials, email proof, highlights.

## Rules
Never edit testimonials to change meaning; disclose incentives; blur private info; get guardian consent for minors.
