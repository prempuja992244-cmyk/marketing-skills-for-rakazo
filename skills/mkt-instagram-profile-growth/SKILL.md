# Instagram profile and growth

## Profile checklist
1. **Account type**: Business or Creator (needed for insights and integrations).
2. **Name field**: brand + main keyword (searchable), e.g. "Riya | Home Baker Pune".
3. **Bio** (150 chars): who you help + result + proof + CTA. Use `mkt-positioning` bios.
4. **Link**: one link page with lead magnet first, then shop/booking/WhatsApp.
5. **Highlights**: Start here · Results/Reviews · Products/Prices · FAQ · Behind the scenes.
6. **Pinned posts (3)**: best-performing Reel, a proof post, an offer/start-here post.
7. **Contact buttons**: call, email, WhatsApp, location for local businesses.

## Growth habits (organic)
- 3–5 Reels/week + 1–2 carousels + daily Stories.
- **Collab posts** with complementary creators/businesses (`mkt-collaborations`) — shown to both audiences.
- Engage 15 minutes/day in the niche: thoughtful comments on relevant creators' posts (by the user or approved drafts — no auto-commenting bots).
- Reply to every comment and DM.
- Turn top-performing topics into series.

## Audit output
Score each item, top 5 fixes, rewritten bio options, highlight plan and pinned post picks.
