# YouTube packaging and SEO

YouTube recommends videos on Home, Suggested, Search, Subscriptions and Shorts separately, aiming to predict what each viewer will watch and be satisfied with. Packaging (title + thumbnail) earns the click; the video must deliver the promise.

## Title (write 10, pick 2)
- ≤ 60 characters visible; main keyword early when search matters.
- Specific promise or curiosity tied to the viewer's goal.
- Title and thumbnail should complement, not repeat, each other.

## Thumbnail concept
One focal point (face with emotion / product / result), ≤ 4 words of text, high contrast, readable on a phone; consistent channel style. Describe it or produce it via `mkt-visual-brief` (1280×720).

## Description
- First 2 lines: the promise + main keyword, and the most important link.
- Then: summary, chapters (00:00 Intro …), resources, related videos/playlist, social links, disclosure if sponsored.
- Chapters help viewers and can appear in Google results.

## Tags and captions
A few tags for spelling variants; upload accurate captions/subtitles; add translated titles/descriptions for big second-language audiences.

## End screen and cards
Point to the next most relevant video or playlist, not a random one.

## A/B testing
If the channel has YouTube's thumbnail/title test feature, test 2–3 options; otherwise swap a weak thumbnail after 48–72 hours if click-through is low versus the channel's norm.

## Apply
Update metadata via the connected YouTube integration only after approval.
