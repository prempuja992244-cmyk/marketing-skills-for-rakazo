# Pinterest

Pinterest works like a visual search engine: Pins can bring traffic for months. Pinterest has indicated static Pins get less reach than before, so mix video and fresh designs.

## Setup
Business account, claim website, profile with keyword name ("Riya | Easy Cake Recipes"), 5–10 boards with keyword titles and descriptions.

## Pin recipe
- Vertical 1000×1500 (2:3), bold title overlay, clean image, brand handle.
- Title with keyword; description 1–3 sentences with natural keywords + CTA.
- Link to a relevant page (blog post, product, lead magnet).
- Video Pins (6–30s) for how-tos and transformations.

## Rhythm
Fresh Pins regularly (e.g. 3–10 per week); make several Pin designs per blog post/product; pin to the most relevant board first.

## Keywords
Use Pinterest search suggestions and trends for the niche; plan seasonal content 1–2 months early (festivals, weddings, back-to-school).

## Automation
Generate Pin designs (`mkt-visual-brief`) and descriptions from each new blog/product; schedule via a connected Pinterest integration after approval.
