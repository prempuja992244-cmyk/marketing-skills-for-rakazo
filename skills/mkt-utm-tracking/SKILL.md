# UTM and attribution

## Naming convention (lowercase, no spaces)
- `utm_source` = platform (youtube, instagram, facebook, linkedin, x, threads, pinterest, newsletter, whatsapp, gbp)
- `utm_medium` = type (social, video, email, bio, story, community, referral)
- `utm_campaign` = campaign or month (diwali-sale-2026, lead-magnet-checklist)
- `utm_content` = specific post or variant (reel-0412-hook-a)

## Practice
- Build links in a sheet (formula concatenation) and shorten with a branded short link if available.
- Different link per platform bio and per major post/email.
- Ask "How did you hear about us?" on forms and at checkout — catches word of mouth and dark social (DM shares).
- In Google Analytics/your shop analytics, review sessions → leads → sales by source/campaign.

## Output
UTM builder sheet, link list per channel, monthly attribution table: source | visits | leads | sales | revenue | notes.
