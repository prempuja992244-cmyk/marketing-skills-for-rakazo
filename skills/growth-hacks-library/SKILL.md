# Growth tactics library (ethical, low cost)

Pick ideas that fit the constraint found in `growth-north-star`, then test them (`mkt-experiments`).

## Acquisition
- Free tool/calculator/template that solves a small problem and links to the offer.
- "Engineering as marketing": quiz or checker people share.
- Answer every question in the niche on Reddit/Quora/Groups (`mkt-community-forums`).
- Partner newsletters/shout-out swaps; collab posts (`mkt-collaborations`).
- Local: free workshop at a café/college, stall at a market, posters with QR to a lead magnet.
- Directory and marketplace listings (`growth-marketplaces`).
- Comment-to-DM lead magnets (`mkt-dm-automation`).
- Turn FAQs into SEO pages and Shorts.

## Activation
- Shorten time to first value: onboarding checklist, starter pack, welcome call/video.
- Personal welcome message within 1 hour.
- Remove form fields and steps; offer WhatsApp ordering.

## Retention
- Reorder reminders timed to usage; loyalty points/stamps; member-only content; monthly "what's new"; win-back message at 45–60 days.

## Referral
- Double-sided rewards (`mkt-referral-program`); shareable results ("my progress" cards); gift a friend a sample.

## Revenue
- Bundles, "most popular" anchor plan, add-ons at checkout, annual/prepay option, upsell after first success.

## Never
Fake scarcity, fake reviews, spam, scraping personal data, dark patterns, impersonation.
