# Tenders, GeM and RFPs

## Opportunity search (routine)
- India: GeM (gem.gov.in) categories and bids, CPPP (eprocure.gov.in), state e-procurement portals, PSU vendor portals.
- Other countries: national procurement portals (e.g. SAM.gov in the US, Find a Tender in the UK), corporate supplier portals.
- Set saved searches/keywords; check 2–3× per week; log: portal | bid ID | title | value | deadline | eligibility | fit.

## Eligibility check
Registration needs (company registration, GST/PAN or local tax IDs, MSME/Udyam status where relevant), turnover and experience thresholds, certifications, EMD/bid security, location requirements.

## Document kit (keep updated)
Company profile, registration certificates, tax documents, financial statements, past work orders/completion certificates, product catalogues/specs, quality certificates, authorisation letters.

## Response
Read the full tender; build a compliance matrix (requirement → our document/answer); prepare technical and price bids; check deadlines and submission format; get owner sign-off.

## Rules
The bot researches, organises and drafts; submissions, digital signatures, payments (EMD/fees) and declarations are done by the authorised person. Never misstate eligibility or experience.
