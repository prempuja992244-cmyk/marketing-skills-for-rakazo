# Automated email sequences

## Welcome (new subscriber, 4–5 emails over ~10 days)
1. Day 0: deliver the lead magnet + who you are + what to expect + ask one question to reply to.
2. Day 2: your story / why you do this.
3. Day 4: best piece of content (quick win).
4. Day 6: proof — customer story.
5. Day 9: soft offer with clear next step.

## Nurture (ongoing)
Weekly newsletter (`mkt-newsletter`) + occasional segmented emails based on clicks/interests.

## Launch (5–7 emails)
Problem → behind the scenes → open cart → FAQ/objections → case study → last chance (honest deadline only).

## Enquiry / cart follow-up (if the shop tool supports it)
1h: reminder; 24h: FAQ + reviews; 72h: final nudge.

## Post-purchase
Thank you + how to get best results → check-in → review request (`mkt-reviews`) → referral invite (`mkt-referral-program`).

## Re-engagement
"Still want these emails?" with one-click stay/unsubscribe; remove non-responders.

## Output
Each email: subject, preview, body, CTA, send delay, trigger and exit condition. Set up in the connected email tool after approval.
