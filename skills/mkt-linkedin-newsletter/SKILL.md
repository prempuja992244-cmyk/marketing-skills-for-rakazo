# LinkedIn newsletter

LinkedIn newsletters notify subscribers (in-app and email), giving distribution that relies less on the feed algorithm.

## Setup
- Name: clear benefit + audience ("Local Growth Notes for Shop Owners").
- Description: what readers get and how often.
- Cadence: weekly or every two weeks — pick what you can sustain.

## Article template (800–1,500 words)
1. Headline with a specific promise; cover image 1920×1080.
2. Opening story or problem (3–5 lines).
3. 3–5 sections with subheads: insight → example → action step.
4. Summary checklist.
5. CTA: reply/comment with a question, download the lead magnet, book a call.

## Promotion
Post a short teaser post linking the issue (link in comment), share key insights as separate posts and carousels during the week.

## Repurpose
Each issue → email newsletter (`mkt-newsletter`), blog post (`mkt-blog-article`), 3–5 social posts.

## Automation
Bot drafts the article from the week's best content and notes; user approves and publishes (article publishing may need the user's browser session if the integration doesn't support it).
