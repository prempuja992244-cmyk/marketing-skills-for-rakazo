# Instagram Reels

Adam Mosseri has said the most important ranking signals are **watch time**, **likes per reach**, and **sends per reach** (people sharing via DM) — sends matter most for reaching new people. Reels and Explore are where you reach non-followers; Feed and Stories keep existing followers.

## Make Reels people watch and send
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds (`mkt-hooks`), on-screen text + spoken line.
- Content worth sending to a friend: relatable moments, useful tips for a specific person, "tag someone who…", surprising facts, local recommendations.
- Content worth saving: checklists, steps, recipes, templates.
- Keep it tight (often 7–45s); for reaching new people stay under ~3 minutes.
- Original audio/voice or trending audio from Instagram's library; original footage (no other apps' watermarks).

## Caption and cover
Caption = hook line + context + question + 3–5 relevant keywords (Instagram search uses keywords). Pick a clean cover that matches the grid.

## Testing
If available on the account, use trial reels (shown first to non-followers) to test hooks/formats without affecting followers. Otherwise post variants on different days.

## After posting
Reply to comments in the first hour; share the Reel to Stories; check per-Reel insights at 24h and 7d: plays, average watch time, shares, saves, follows.

## Automation
Draft captions and schedule via the connected Instagram (Business/Creator) integration or a scheduler (`mkt-scheduler`); publish after approval unless auto-publish is approved.
