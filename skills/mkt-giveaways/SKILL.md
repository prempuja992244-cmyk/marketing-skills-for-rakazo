# Giveaways and contests

## Prize
Your own product/service (attracts buyers, not freebie hunters) or a partner bundle. Avoid generic prizes (phones, cash) that attract the wrong audience.

## Mechanics (simple)
Follow + comment answering a question about the product/need (gives you insight) + optional share to Story. Keep it 3–7 days. Partner giveaways: both accounts post a collab post.

## Rules text (always include)
Organiser, eligibility (age, location), dates/time zone, how to enter, how the winner is chosen (random or judged with criteria), prize details, how winners are contacted, statement that the platform doesn't sponsor/endorse it (Instagram/Facebook requirement), privacy note.

## After
Announce winner publicly, deliver prize, DM all entrants a small thank-you offer, follow up new followers with best content. Measure follower retention 30 days later.

## Rules
Don't require tagging in ways platforms prohibit (e.g. tagging people in photos they're not in); check local laws on lotteries/contests; no fake entries or rigged results.
