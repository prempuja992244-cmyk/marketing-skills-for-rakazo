# Pitch decks and company profiles

## Partner / client deck (10–12 slides)
1. Title + one-line value proposition
2. Their problem/opportunity (in their terms)
3. Our solution and how it works
4. Proof: results, clients, testimonials, numbers
5. Why us (differentiators)
6. Offer/partnership model and what they get
7. Implementation/timeline
8. Commercials (if appropriate)
9. Team
10. Next steps

## Investor deck (12–15 slides)
Problem · solution · market size (`bd-market-expansion`) · product · traction · business model · go-to-market · competition · team · financials summary · ask and use of funds.

## One-pager / capability statement
Who we are · what we do · key clients/projects · capabilities and certifications · differentiators · contact. Fits on one page.

## Writing rules
One message per slide, numbers over adjectives, real logos/testimonials only with permission, consistent brand design (`mkt-visual-brief`).

## Output
Slide-by-slide copy and visual notes; create the deck with the bot's document/slide tools; intro email draft to send it.
