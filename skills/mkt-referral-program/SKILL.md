# Referral and word of mouth

## Make the experience share-worthy first
Surprise moments (handwritten note, extra sample, fast fix), easy-to-share packaging/results, a story worth telling.

## Program design
- **Who**: customers after a success moment (not just after payment).
- **Reward** (low cash cost): store credit, free add-on, upgrade, priority access, donation in their name; double-sided (friend gets something too).
- **Mechanism**: unique code or link; track in a sheet/CRM or the shop tool.
- **Ask**: "Know someone who'd love this? Share your link — you both get {reward}."

## Touchpoints
Thank-you page, delivery message, post-purchase email (`mkt-email-sequences`), WhatsApp follow-up, review reply, packaging insert.

## Measure
Referral share rate, referred customers, cost per referred customer, repeat referrers (make them ambassadors).

## Rules
Clear terms; honour rewards; no spammy forced sharing; comply with local consumer and advertising rules.
