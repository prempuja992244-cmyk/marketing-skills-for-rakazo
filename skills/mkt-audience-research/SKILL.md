# Audience research (free sources)

## Sources to mine (use the bot's browser and connected apps)
- Your own comments, DMs, emails, reviews and sales calls.
- Competitor comments on YouTube/Instagram/Facebook (what people praise, complain about, ask).
- Reviews: Google Maps, Amazon/Flipkart, app stores, G2, Justdial.
- Reddit and Quora threads; Facebook Groups in the niche.
- Google autocomplete, "People also ask", related searches; YouTube search suggestions.

## Extract into a voice-of-customer bank
For each insight record: exact quote | source link | type (pain / desire / question / objection / language) | frequency.

## Build
1. **Top 10 pains** and **top 10 desires** in the customer's own words.
2. **Top 30 questions** people ask → each becomes a content idea.
3. **Objections** that stop buying → content that answers them.
4. **Content preferences**: formats and creators they already watch.
5. **Personas** (2–3): name, situation, goal, frustration, where they scroll, what makes them share.

## Save
`audience.md` with the bank, personas and a list of 30 ready content ideas mapped to pains/questions.

## Rules
Quote only public content, never collect personal data beyond what's needed; don't contact people found during research without the user's approval.
