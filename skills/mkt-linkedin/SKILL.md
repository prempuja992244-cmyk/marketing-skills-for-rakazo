# LinkedIn organic growth

Reports through 2026 consistently show personal profiles get far more feed distribution than company pages, posts with outbound links in the body get less reach, and document/carousel and text posts perform strongly.

## Profile
Headline (who you help + outcome), banner with promise, About (problem → approach → proof → CTA), Featured (lead magnet, best post), creator mode topics if available.

## Post formats
- **Story/lesson text post** (1,200–2,000 chars): hook line, short lines, specific details, lesson, question.
- **Document carousel** (`mkt-carousel`): frameworks, checklists, case studies.
- **Native video** (vertical/square, captions): tips, behind the scenes.
- **Opinion post**: clear stance on an industry topic with reasons.

## Rules for reach
- Stay on one niche topic area so LinkedIn knows who to show you to.
- Put links in comments or edit them in later, not in the main post.
- Don't heavily edit in the first hour; reply to every comment with substance.
- Comment thoughtfully on 5–10 niche posts daily (user does it or approves drafts).
- 3–5 posts per week; consistency over volume.
- Real experience beats generic AI text — add specific numbers, names (with permission), mistakes.

## Company page
Use it for hiring, proof and updates; grow reach by having founders/team share in their own words (employee advocacy) with ready drafts.

## Automation
Draft posts/comments; publish via the connected LinkedIn integration after approval.
