# Positioning and messaging

## Work through
1. **Alternatives**: what customers would do if you didn't exist (competitor, DIY, do nothing).
2. **Unique attributes**: what you have that alternatives don't (speed, local, price model, expertise, guarantee, story).
3. **Value**: what each attribute means for the customer (outcome, not feature).
4. **Best-fit customer**: who cares most about that value.
5. **Category/frame**: how you describe yourself so people instantly get it.

## Outputs
- One-line positioning: *For {customer} who {pain}, {brand} is the {category} that {key outcome}, unlike {alternative}.*
- Elevator pitch (30 words).
- 3 message pillars with proof for each.
- Profile bios sized per platform: Instagram (150 chars), X (160), YouTube channel description first 2 lines, LinkedIn headline (220), Facebook Page intro (255), WhatsApp channel description.
- 5 taglines to test.

## Rules
Claims must be provable from `brand-context.md`. Avoid empty words (best, leading, world-class) unless backed by proof. Save to `brand-context.md` under Positioning.
