# Social listening

## Track
Brand name and misspellings, product names, founder name, branded hashtag, competitor names, category keywords + buying phrases ("recommend a", "looking for", "anyone know a … in {city}").

## Sources
Platform searches (X, Threads, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook public posts/Groups you're in), Reddit/Quora, Google Alerts / news search, review sites, connected listening tools if any.

## Routine (daily or 3×/week)
1. Search each term for the last 24–72h.
2. Log: date | source | link | type (mention/lead/complaint/UGC/question/competitor news) | sentiment | action.
3. Actions: reply/thank (drafts), lead to sales, UGC permission request (`mkt-ugc`), complaint to the user, content idea to `audience.md`.
4. Weekly summary: volume, sentiment, top themes, opportunities.

## Rules
Engage only where helpful and appropriate; don't jump into unrelated conversations to self-promote; respect privacy (no profiling of individuals).
