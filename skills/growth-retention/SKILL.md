# Retention and repeat business

Keeping a customer usually costs far less than finding a new one, and retained customers refer others.

## Diagnose
- Repeat rate: % of customers who buy again within a normal cycle (30/60/90 days).
- Cohorts: of customers from month X, how many are active in month X+1, X+2…
- Ask lost customers why (one-question message).

## Levers
1. **Onboarding**: help them get results fast (guide, check-in on day 3/7).
2. **Reminders**: timed to product usage (refill, service due, seasonal).
3. **Loyalty**: points/stamp cards, member prices, birthday offer, VIP early access.
4. **Personal touch**: handwritten note, name-based messages, follow-up after delivery.
5. **Content for customers**: tips to use the product better (email/WhatsApp).
6. **Win-back**: message at 45–60 days of inactivity with a reason to return.
7. **Feedback loop**: NPS/1-question survey → fix top complaint → tell customers you fixed it.

## Automation
Bot builds the customer list from the shop/CRM/sheet, segments (new, active, at-risk, lost), drafts messages per segment, and schedules them after approval; monthly retention report.

## Rules
Opt-in messaging only; easy opt-out; no spammy frequency.
