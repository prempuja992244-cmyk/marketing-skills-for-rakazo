# Reviews and reputation

## Ask (ethically)
- Timing: right after a happy moment (delivery, result, compliment).
- Channels: in person + QR card, WhatsApp/SMS/email with a direct review link, receipt/invoice footer, post-purchase email (`mkt-email-sequences`).
- Message: short, personal, one link. "Could you share a quick review of your experience? It really helps a small business like ours: {link}"
- Ask every customer, not only happy ones (selective asking/"review gating" breaks Google's policies).

## Reply
- **Positive**: thank by name, mention the specific thing, invite back. Vary wording.
- **Negative**: reply calmly within 48h, apologise for the experience, state facts briefly, take it offline (phone/email), fix it; don't argue or share private details.
- **Fake/abusive**: report through the platform; reply briefly and professionally.

## Use reviews
Turn reviews into posts, Stories, website testimonials, ad-free proof in emails (with permission where names/faces are shown).

## Automation
Daily: fetch new reviews from connected platforms → draft replies → user approves (auto-post thank-you replies only if approved). Weekly: send review requests to last week's customers.

## Rules
Never buy reviews, write fake ones, or offer incentives for positive reviews (against platform policies and consumer law in many countries).
