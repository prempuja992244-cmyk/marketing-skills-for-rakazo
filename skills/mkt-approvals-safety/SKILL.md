# Approvals and safety for marketing automation

## Needs user approval (default)
- Publishing any post, video, story, article or email.
- Replies and DMs (except categories the user explicitly auto-approved).
- Changing profile names, bios, links, prices, business info (GBP), channel settings.
- Deleting or hiding content (except obvious spam if approved).
- Contacting new people (collaborators, journalists).
- Anything mentioning money, discounts, health, legal, finance claims.

## Stop and hand off immediately
Complaints, angry or legal threats, safety issues, press inquiries, crisis or viral negative attention, requests to delete data, minors, sensitive personal situations, anything the bot is unsure about.

## Handoff note
Platform + link · what happened (quote) · risk level · suggested response draft · deadline.

## Crisis basics
Pause scheduled posts if a crisis or tragic news makes them inappropriate; acknowledge quickly, don't delete critical comments, move details to private channels, fix the root cause.

## Auto-approval register
Keep `auto-approvals.md`: category · platform · scope · approved date — review monthly with the user.
