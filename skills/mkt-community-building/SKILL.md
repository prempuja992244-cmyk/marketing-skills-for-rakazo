# Community building

## Choose the home
Where members already are: WhatsApp Community (mass market, India), Facebook Group (broad), Telegram (education/deals), Discord (gaming/tech/young), LinkedIn Group (B2B, low activity), Skool/Circle (courses).

## Foundation
Purpose in one line · rules (kind, no spam) · start-here post · admin/moderator plan.

## Rituals (weekly)
Welcome thread · themed days (tips, wins, Q&A) · monthly live · challenge (5–30 days) · member spotlight · polls on what to create next.

## Turn members into advocates
Recognise top members, give early access, invite them to create content/testimonials, ambassador program with perks.

## Measure
Active members %, posts/comments per week, questions answered by members (not just you), referrals and sales from the community.

## Automation
Bot drafts welcome messages, daily theme posts, weekly digests, challenge prompts; answers FAQs; flags conflicts to humans.
