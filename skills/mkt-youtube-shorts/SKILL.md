# YouTube Shorts

Shorts are a major discovery surface (Metricool reports about 6 in 10 organic YouTube views come from the Shorts feed). Viewers swipe through a feed, so the first seconds and completion decide reach.

## What to optimise
- **Viewed vs swiped away** (in YouTube Analytics) → hook strength.
- **Average % viewed / loops** → pacing and payoff.
- **Subscribers gained** → series and channel promise.

## Playbook
1. Make **series** (same format, new episode) so viewers know why to subscribe.
2. Hook in 1–2s, payoff before the end, loop the ending into the start when natural.
3. 20–45s default; vertical; captions on screen.
4. Link a **related long video** from the Short to move viewers into long-form.
5. Post consistently (e.g. 3–7 per week) and review weekly; double down on formats with high viewed-vs-swiped.
6. Cut Shorts from long videos (`mkt-repurpose`) — each must stand alone.

## Publishing (automation)
Upload via the connected YouTube integration with title (hook + keyword), short description, related video link, and #Shorts if helpful. Schedule through `mkt-scheduler`. Draft first; publish after approval unless auto-publish was approved for this series.

## Rules
Original or licensed audio only; label realistic AI-generated content when YouTube requires; no reuploads of others' clips without transformation and rights.
