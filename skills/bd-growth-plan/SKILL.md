# Quarterly growth plan (OKRs)

## 1. Where we are
Revenue, customers, average order, repeat rate, main channels, capacity, cash constraints (from the user and `growth-north-star`).

## 2. Objective and key results (1–2 objectives, 3–4 KRs each)
Example: *Objective:* Become the go-to home bakery in our city. *KRs:* 120 orders/month (from 70); 80 new Google reviews; 1,500 WhatsApp channel followers; 2 corporate gifting partners.

## 3. Initiatives per KR
Map each KR to skills: organic content (`mkt-content-strategy`), local SEO (`mkt-local-seo`), partnerships (`bd-partnerships`), retention (`growth-retention`), marketplaces (`growth-marketplaces`), sales follow-up (sales plugin if installed).

## 4. Weekly scorecard
5–8 leading indicators: posts published, leads, enquiries replied < 1h, meetings, reviews requested, partner calls, orders. Bot fills it every Monday from connected data.

## 5. Cadence
Weekly 30-minute review (scorecard + top 3 actions), monthly experiment review, quarterly reset.

## Output
`growth-plan-Q{n}.md` with objectives, KRs, initiatives, owners, scorecard sheet, and calendar of reviews; set up the weekly scorecard routine after approval.
