# X (Twitter)

On X, replies and conversations drive reach, recommendations are interest-based, and posts and replies from mutuals have been prioritised. X has published its ranking code, so behaviour that sparks real conversation is what's rewarded.

## Profile
Clear photo, name + niche keyword, bio with promise + proof, pinned post (best thread or lead magnet), link.

## Post types
- **Single insight**: one sharp idea, specific and quotable.
- **Thread** (5–10 posts): hook post with promise, one point per post, final post with summary + CTA.
- **Hot take / opinion** with reasons (stay respectful).
- **Build in public**: numbers, wins, failures.
- **Question / poll** to start conversations.

## Reply strategy (biggest lever for small accounts)
Spend 20–30 minutes daily writing thoughtful replies to larger accounts in your niche — add data, a story, or a counterpoint. The user writes or approves; no auto-reply bots.

## Rhythm
1–3 posts/day + replies; reply to everyone who replies to you early.

## Avoid
Engagement bait spam, mass tagging, reposting the same text, aggressive links in every post (put links in a reply), automated follows/unfollows.

## Automation
Draft posts and threads; schedule via the connected X integration or scheduler after approval.
