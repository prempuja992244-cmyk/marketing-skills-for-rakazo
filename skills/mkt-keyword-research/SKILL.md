# Keyword research (free methods)

## Steps
1. **Seeds**: products, problems, audience words from `audience.md`, locations.
2. **Expand** with free sources (via browser): Google autocomplete (add a–z), People Also Ask, related searches, YouTube autocomplete, Amazon/Flipkart suggestions, Reddit/Quora titles, Google Trends; Google Search Console queries if connected.
3. **Intent** for each term: informational (how/what/why), commercial (best/vs/review), transactional (price/buy/near me), local (city/near me).
4. **Cluster** related terms into topics — one page/video per cluster, not per keyword.
5. **Prioritise**: relevance to the offer × intent × ability to rank (look at who ranks: forums/small sites = opportunity; big brands only = harder).
6. **Map** clusters to formats: blog guide, product/service page, local page, YouTube video, FAQ.

## Output
Table: cluster | main keyword | variations | intent | current top results (type) | format | priority (H/M/L). Save as `keywords.md`.

## Rules
Write for people first; keywords guide topics and headings, not stuffing.
