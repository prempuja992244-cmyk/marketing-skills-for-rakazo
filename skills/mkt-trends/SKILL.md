# Trends and moments

## Where to look (daily/weekly scan)
Instagram Reels trending audio and Professional Dashboard, YouTube Shorts trending sounds, X/Threads trending topics, Google Trends (country/region), news in the niche, festival and event calendar, competitor and creator feeds.

## Filter (all must be yes)
Relevant to our audience? Can we add our own angle/expertise? Safe for the brand (no tragedy, politics, or controversy we don't want)? Can we post within 24–72 hours (trend window)?

## Adapt
Trend format + our niche insight: e.g. trending "POV" format → "POV: you finally priced your cakes right". Keep the brand's voice and add value.

## Seasonal calendar
Plan 4–6 weeks ahead for big moments relevant to the audience (e.g. New Year, Holi, Ramadan/Eid, back-to-school, monsoon, Diwali, wedding season, Black Friday, Christmas) and industry dates.

## Rules
Use trending audio only from platform libraries (commercial accounts may have restricted libraries); never mock tragedies; avoid copyrighted characters and memes with unclear rights for brand accounts.
