# YouTube channel setup and audit

## Checklist
1. **Niche and promise**: one sentence — who it's for and what they get ("Easy home-bakery business tips for beginners in Hindi").
2. **Name/handle**: short, searchable, matches other socials.
3. **Banner**: promise + upload schedule; important text inside the safe area.
4. **Description**: first 2 lines state the promise with main keywords; then topics, schedule, contact email, links.
5. **Channel trailer** (for non-subscribers, 30–60s) and **featured video** for returning viewers.
6. **Playlists** per pillar/series with keyword titles — they keep viewers watching your next video.
7. **Links**: website, lead magnet, WhatsApp channel, Instagram.
8. **Upload defaults**: category, language, default description footer with links and chapters template, tags.
9. **Series formats**: 2–3 repeatable formats (e.g. "Mistake Monday", "₹0 marketing tip") that viewers recognise.
10. **Shorts ↔ long-form funnel**: plan Shorts that introduce topics covered in depth in long videos; link the related video in Shorts.

## Audit output
Score each item (OK / fix), top 5 fixes ordered by impact, and drafted text for description, banner copy and playlist titles. Apply changes through the connected YouTube integration only after user approval.
