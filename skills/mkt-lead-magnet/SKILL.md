# Lead magnets

## Good lead magnet = specific + quick win + leads to the paid offer
Ideas: checklist, swipe file/templates, price calculator, 5-day email/WhatsApp challenge, mini video course, buyer's guide, recipe pack, local resource list, free audit/sample, quiz with personalised result.

## Pick
Score ideas on: solves a top pain (`audience.md`), consumable in < 15 minutes, naturally leads to the product, easy to make this week.

## Build
1. Title: "{Result} in {time} without {pain}" style.
2. Content: short, practical, branded; end with a clear next step (book / buy / reply).
3. Format: PDF/Doc/Sheet/video; produce with the bot's document tools.
4. Landing page copy (`mkt-landing-page`): headline, 3 bullets of what they get, form, proof.
5. Delivery: instant email (welcome sequence) or DM/WhatsApp.

## Promote
Bio links, pinned posts, end of videos, comment keyword, Facebook Group, blog CTAs.

## Measure
Visitors → sign-ups (%), sign-ups → customers (%). Improve the weaker step.
