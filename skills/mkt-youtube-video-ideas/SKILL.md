# YouTube video ideas (validated)

## Sources
1. **YouTube search autocomplete** for niche seed words (search via connected YouTube app or browser).
2. **Outliers**: videos in similar-size channels that got far more views than that channel's average — the topic/packaging is proven.
3. **Audience questions**: comments on your and competitor videos, `audience.md`.
4. **Google Trends / seasonal** moments (festivals, exam results, budgets, tax deadlines).
5. **Your analytics**: top videos by views and by subscribers gained → make follow-ups.

## Validate each idea
- Is there a clear viewer (persona) and a clear promise?
- Proven demand (search or outlier) ?
- Can we make it better: more specific, more recent, local, clearer, stronger proof?
- Can the title + thumbnail sell it in < 2 seconds?

## Output
Table: idea | target viewer | proof of demand (links) | angle that beats existing videos | working title | thumbnail concept | Short to promote it. Pick the top 5.

## Rules
Don't copy titles/thumbnails verbatim; take the topic, add your own angle and experience.
