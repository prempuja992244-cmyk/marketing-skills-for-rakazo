# Marketplaces and free listings

## Choose by business type
- **Products**: Google Merchant Center free listings, Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Etsy (handmade), Instagram/Facebook Shop, ONDC network apps (India).
- **Local services**: Google Business Profile, Justdial, Sulekha, UrbanCompany (if eligible), Practo (clinics), Zomato/Swiggy (food).
- **B2B/manufacturers**: IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Alibaba, industry directories, GeM for government buyers (`bd-tenders-gem`).
- **Software/digital**: Product Hunt, G2/Capterra, app/plugin marketplaces, AI tool directories.

## Listing optimisation
Keyword-rich title (as buyers search), clear photos/video, benefits-first bullets, complete specs, competitive but profitable price (include fees), fast replies, policies, and reviews (`mkt-reviews`).

## Operations
Track inventory and orders in one sheet; respond to buyer questions within hours; monitor ratings; fix negative patterns quickly.

## Measure
Listing views → clicks → orders, fees %, margin per channel. Double down on channels with healthy margin.

## Rules
Follow each marketplace's policies (no fake reviews, no keyword stuffing, accurate listings).
