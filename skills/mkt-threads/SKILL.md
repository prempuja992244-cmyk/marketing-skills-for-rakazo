# Threads

Threads' feed has been less saturated than X or LinkedIn, so small accounts can still get real reach; its tone is more curious and conversational than X's.

## What works
- Conversation starters: questions, honest opinions, "we tried X — here's what happened", mini teardowns.
- Short personal updates and lessons; behind-the-scenes.
- One relevant topic tag per post to help discovery.
- Replying to others' posts in your niche before and after you post (15–20 minutes).
- Early engagement — post when your audience is active and reply quickly.

## Don't
Paste X posts as-is (tone mismatch); post only links; spam tags.

## Rhythm
1–3 posts/day is fine; prioritise replies and conversation threads.

## Automation
Draft posts in the brand voice adapted from other content (`mkt-repurpose`); publish via the connected integration or scheduler after approval.
