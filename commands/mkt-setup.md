Set this bot up as my organic (zero ad spend) marketing assistant. Extra notes: $ARGUMENTS

1. Run `mkt-brand-context` to collect and save my brand, audience, voice, language, channels and 90-day goal.
2. Run `mkt-audience-research` (quick version) and `mkt-content-strategy` to choose my focus channels, pillars and cadence.
3. Run `mkt-connect-accounts`: check which of my accounts (Gmail, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, Search Console, Sheets) are connected and tell me exactly what to connect in Integrations. Never ask for my passwords.
4. Propose the routines from `mkt-daily-routine` in my time zone and ask me to approve before creating them.
5. Finish with a 5-line summary: what's ready, what I must do, and the first week's plan.
